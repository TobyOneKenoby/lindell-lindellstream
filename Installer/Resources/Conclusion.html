Installation complete

Open REAPER and scan for new VST3 plug-ins if needed. Insert Lindell Streams Live last on your stereo master bus.

Use the two PASTE buttons to enter your playlist share link and private connection key. Click CONNECT, verify the playlist name, then GO LIVE.

Listeners use the playlist's Live panel. Closing the plug-in window does not end a broadcast; use STOP BROADCAST.

The plug-in is installed in:
/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

Instructions and third-party notices are in:
/Library/Application Support/Lindell Streams Live/
