Lindell Streams Live

VST3 beta · Version 0.3.1

Stream your stereo mix directly from REAPER to a Lindell Streams playlist.

For Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 12 or newer.

Quit REAPER and other audio applications before continuing.

This installer places the VST3 in the standard system plug-in folder. Administrator authorization is required. An existing copy at that location is updated.

A playlist share link and its separate private connection key are required to broadcast. Neither is included in this installer.
